\section{Tournament simulation}
A Monte-Carlo engine simulates the full tournament thousands of times. Each
run:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Samples a result for every group-stage fixture.
  \item Applies FIFA tiebreak ordering (points, goal difference, goals scored).
  \item Selects the eight best-third teams.
  \item Builds a deterministic Round-of-32 bracket.
  \item Simulates knockout matches with a penalty-proxy adjustment for ties.
\end{enumerate}
Aggregations yield qualification probabilities, round-reached probabilities,
championship probabilities and most likely bracket paths.
