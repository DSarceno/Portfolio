\section{Models}
Three model families produce outcome probabilities:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Multinomial logistic regression} on standardized features.
  \item \textbf{XGBoost} multi-class classifier with shallow trees, modest
        learning rate, and 400 estimators.
  \item \textbf{Poisson} scoreline model with Dixon-Coles low-score
        adjustment, providing exact-score probabilities and a scoreline-derived
        H/D/A probability.
\end{itemize}
Probabilities are calibrated with isotonic regression (one-vs-rest) before
being passed to a weighted blender that combines them with rating-based
probabilities.
