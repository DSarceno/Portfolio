\section{Team strength ratings}
We combine three complementary rating systems:
\paragraph{Elo.} Competition-aware $K$ factor (higher for World Cup matches),
margin-of-victory multiplier, and configurable home advantage. The Elo system
provides a stable backbone of long-horizon team strength.
\paragraph{PI rating.} Maintains separate home and away ratings, updated via a
log-scale expected goal difference (Constantinou \& Fenton, 2013). Particularly
effective in capturing teams that travel poorly.
\paragraph{Rolling form.} Sliding window of points and goal differences over
the last $n$ matches, capturing very recent shifts in team quality (injuries,
managerial change).
\paragraph{Ensemble.} The three ratings are z-scored and weighted into a
composite strength index used as backbone probabilities.
