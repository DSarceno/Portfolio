\section{Evaluation}
We evaluate the system using log-loss, Brier score, accuracy, macro F1 and
expected calibration error, plus quiniela-specific metrics (correct 1X2,
correct exact score, upset hit rate, simulated first-place rate in a 100-entry
pool). Backtesting is performed via rolling-origin cross-validation on
historical international matches, with strict temporal splits to prevent
information leakage. Calibration plots and feature-importance reports are
exported under \texttt{outputs/diagnostics/}.
