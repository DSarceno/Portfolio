\section{Quiniela strategy}
The strategy layer translates calibrated probabilities into picks under four
risk profiles:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Safe.} High favorite threshold, conservative draws.
  \item \textbf{Balanced.} Expected-value pick with a draw fallback when
        favorites are weak.
  \item \textbf{Aggressive.} Triggers upsets when the upset-window and
        favorite-fragility scores agree; targets draw value when entropy is
        high.
  \item \textbf{Contrarian.} Fades public bias and reputation; pursues pool
        differentiation.
\end{itemize}
Each pick is exported with confidence, rationale, and (optional) recommended
scoreline.
