\section{Conclusion}
The presented system is designed to win quinielas, not merely to forecast.
Probability modelling and pick selection are decoupled, the tournament state
is updated after every matchday, and four risk profiles allow the user to
match strategy to pool dynamics. Limitations remain: penalty shootouts are
modelled via a Bernoulli proxy, travel features are coarse, and StatsBomb
event data is sparse for non-elite competitions. Future work will integrate
live odds feeds, player-level injury feeds, and pool-specific scoring
optimisation.
