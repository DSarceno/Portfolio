\section{Introduction}
The 2026 FIFA World Cup expands the tournament to 48 teams across 12 groups
and 104 matches. Traditional academic forecasting systems optimise log-loss or
Brier score, which yields well-calibrated probabilities but does not directly
maximise quiniela performance. Quinielas reward correct picks, exact scorelines
and (often) unique upset selections, and they penalise herd behaviour. This
report describes a system designed end-to-end to optimise quiniela outcomes:
forecasting (probability) and strategy (pick selection) are deliberately
decoupled, enabling risk-tiered pick sheets.
