\begin{abstract}
We describe an aggressive, tournament-aware forecasting and pick-optimization
system for the 2026 FIFA World Cup. The system combines Elo, PI, and rolling-form
ratings; multinomial and gradient-boosted classifiers; a Dixon-Coles-adjusted
Poisson scoreline model; and a dedicated quiniela strategy layer separating
\emph{true probability} from \emph{best pick}.
\end{abstract}
