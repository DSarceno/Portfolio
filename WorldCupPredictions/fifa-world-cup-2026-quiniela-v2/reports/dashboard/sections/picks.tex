\section*{Picks per risk profile}
Pick sheets are produced by the strategy layer using calibrated probabilities
and the auxiliary aggressive-strategy features (upset window, favorite
fragility, public bias, draw trap).

\paragraph{Safe.} Highest single-result probability. Conservative draws.
\paragraph{Balanced.} Best expected-value pick. Draw fallback for low-margin
matches.
\paragraph{Aggressive.} Upset trigger when both upset-window and fragility
agree; draw value when entropy is high.
\paragraph{Contrarian.} Fade public bias. Prioritise pool differentiation.

The CSV exports live in \texttt{outputs/picks/quiniela\_safe.csv},
\texttt{outputs/picks/quiniela\_balanced.csv},
\texttt{outputs/picks/quiniela\_aggressive.csv} and
\texttt{outputs/picks/quiniela\_contrarian.csv}.
