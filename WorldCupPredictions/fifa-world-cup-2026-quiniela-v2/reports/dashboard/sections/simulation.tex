\section*{Simulation snapshot}
Tournament Monte-Carlo aggregates live under \texttt{outputs/simulations/}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{tournament\_probabilities.csv} -- group-stage qualification
        probabilities.
  \item \texttt{championship\_probabilities.csv} -- per-team championship
        probability.
  \item \texttt{round\_reached\_probabilities.csv} -- probability of reaching
        each round.
  \item \texttt{bracket\_paths.csv} -- the run-level champion, runner-up,
        third-place sequence.
\end{itemize}
Re-run \texttt{python scripts/simulate\_tournament.py} (or \texttt{make simulate})
to refresh after a matchday update.
