# User Guide — LinkedIn Topic-to-Growth System

This guide is for **non-technical users**. It explains how to use the tool, what each
question means, and how to turn the output into published LinkedIn posts. You do not need to
write any code.

---

## Who This Tool Is For

Anyone who wants to grow on LinkedIn around a specific topic: founders, freelancers, job
seekers, engineers, scientists, consultants, educators, and creators. If you have a subject
you know well and want a consistent stream of credible posts, this tool is for you.

## What You Get

- A **strategy** for how to position yourself.
- **Content pillars** (the recurring themes you'll post about).
- A **calendar** telling you what to post and when.
- **Ready-to-copy posts written in Spanish.**
- An **image idea (prompt)** for each post.
- A **growth plan**: when to post, how to comment, network, and measure results.

## How to Start

1. Open this project in Claude Code.
2. Type a request such as:
   > "Use the LinkedIn growth skill. My topic is *home-cooked meal prep for busy parents*."
3. Claude will ask you ten questions. Answer them in a single message (or say *"use the
   defaults"* and Claude will fill in sensible choices and tell you what it assumed).

## The Ten Questions — and Why They're Asked

Answering specifically is the single biggest lever on quality. Vague answers produce
generic posts.

| # | Question | Why it matters | A weak answer | A strong answer |
|---|----------|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | What is the general topic? | Everything is built from it. | "Marketing" | "Email marketing for online course creators" |
| 2 | Main goal? | Sets the calls-to-action and angle. | "Grow" | "Get 5 coaching clients per month" |
| 3 | Target audience? | Controls vocabulary and examples. | "Everyone" | "Solo course creators doing <$10k/mo" |
| 4 | Your LinkedIn level? | Sets a realistic plan. | — | "Beginner, ~200 connections" |
| 5 | How often to post? | Defines calendar size. | — | "3 times per week" |
| 6 | Tone? | Shapes the voice. | — | "Educational but bold" |
| 7 | Beginner / expert / mixed? | Controls depth. | — | "Mixed" |
| 8 | Which formats? | Drives the calendar + images. | — | "Carousels and text" |
| 9 | What offer to support? | Anchors the selling. | — | "My $300 email course" |
| 10 | Anything to avoid? | Hard limits. | — | "No politics, no crypto talk" |

## How to Provide the Best Inputs

- **Name a real audience**, ideally one person you can picture.
- **State your offer plainly**, even if it's just "my personal brand."
- **List anything off-limits** so it never appears.
- If unsure about an optional answer, say so — the defaults are sensible.

## How to Interpret the Generated Strategy

- **Strategy Summary** = your positioning in a nutshell. Check it sounds like you.
- **Content Pillars** = your recurring themes. If one feels off-brand, ask to swap it.
- **Chronogram** = your calendar. Each row is one post with its hook, format, goal, CTA,
  and image idea.
- **Full Posts** = copy-ready text in Spanish.
- **Image Prompts** = paste into an AI image generator to create the visual.
- **Growth Strategy** = your weekly routine.
- **Assumptions** = anything Claude guessed. Correct these if wrong.

## How to Edit the Generated Content

- Edit posts directly to match your voice — the output is a strong first draft, not a final.
- Keep the hook punchy; trim anything that doesn't add value.
- Swap examples for real ones from your own experience whenever possible.

## How to Regenerate or Refine Outputs

Just ask in plain language:

- "Make the posts more technical."
- "Rewrite post 3 with a storytelling tone."
- "Give me 5 alternative hooks for post 1."
- "Change the calendar to weekly instead of 3x per week."
- "Redo the image prompt for post 2 in a photo style."

## Recommended LinkedIn Publishing Workflow

1. Pick the week's posts from the calendar.
2. Edit each into your own voice.
3. Generate the images from the prompts.
4. Schedule or publish on the planned days.
5. Reply to comments within the first 1–2 hours.
6. At week's end, review the metrics and tell Claude what worked.

## Recommended Review Process

Before publishing each post, confirm: strong hook, clear single idea, concrete value, real
(not invented) claims, a clear call-to-action, and that it sounds like you.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Posting and disappearing — engagement in the first hour drives reach.
- Selling in every post — keep it to about one in five.
- Copy-pasting without editing — add your voice and real examples.
- Trusting general posting times forever — check your own analytics after a few weeks.
- Vague intake answers — they produce vague posts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will it post to LinkedIn for me?** No. It creates the posts and plan; you publish them.

**Will it create the images?** No. It writes image *prompts*; you generate the images with
your preferred AI image tool.

**Why are the posts in Spanish?** That's the configured output language for this project.
You can ask for another language, but Spanish is the default.

**Can I get a 90-day plan?** Yes — just ask for the period you want.

**What if my topic is broad?** Claude will ask one question to narrow it first.

**Can it invent statistics to make posts stronger?** No. It won't fabricate facts. If a
claim isn't sourced, it's reframed as your own reasoning or experience.

**I skipped some questions — is that okay?** Yes. Claude uses sensible defaults and lists
what it assumed so you can correct it.
