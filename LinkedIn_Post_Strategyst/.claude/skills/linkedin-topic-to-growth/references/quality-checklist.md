# Quality-Control Checklist

Run **every** post through this gate before presenting it. If a post fails any item, fix it
and re-check. Do not present failing posts.

## Per-Post Gate

Every post must:

- [ ] **Start with a strong hook** — the first line stops the scroll and is specific.
- [ ] **Have a clear audience** — a defined reader would recognize themselves.
- [ ] **Give concrete value** — a usable idea, step, or insight (not vague advice).
- [ ] **Avoid generic advice** — nothing that could be copy-pasted to any niche.
- [ ] **Use readable formatting** — short lines, white space, mobile-friendly.
- [ ] **Express one clear idea** — single focus, no topic-stuffing.
- [ ] **End with a meaningful CTA** — one specific action tied to the post's goal.
- [ ] **Avoid unsupported claims** — no invented stats/studies/quotes; claims sourced or
      reframed as the author's reasoning and labeled.
- [ ] **Not sound like spam** — no hashtag stuffing, no engagement-bait, no fake urgency.
- [ ] **Sound human and credible** — natural voice in the chosen tone, in Spanish.

## System-Level Gate

Across the whole output:

- [ ] **Language:** all post copy is in Spanish (scaffolding may be in the user's language).
- [ ] **Pillar coverage:** posts map to the defined pillars; mix matches the goal.
- [ ] **Hook variety:** no repeated hook structures across the calendar.
- [ ] **Format variety:** honors the user's `formats` choice.
- [ ] **Offer balance:** overt selling ≈ ≤ 1 in 5 posts.
- [ ] **Constraints honored:** nothing from `avoid` appears anywhere.
- [ ] **Assumptions:** every assumption is listed explicitly in the Assumptions block.
- [ ] **Specificity:** each post is unmistakably about the user's topic and audience.

## If a Check Fails

| Failure | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Weak hook | Rewrite line 1 with a specific tension, number, or contrarian claim. |
| Generic advice | Add a concrete example, step, or number from the user's domain. |
| Two ideas | Split into two posts; keep one idea each. |
| Unsupported stat | Remove, source honestly, or reframe as experience/opinion (labeled). |
| Salesy | Move to soft CTA; ensure ≤ 1 in 5 posts sell overtly. |
| English copy | Translate to natural Spanish (not literal). |
