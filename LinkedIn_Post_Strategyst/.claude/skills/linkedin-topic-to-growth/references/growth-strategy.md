# LinkedIn Growth Strategy

Deliver this as section F. Tailor every recommendation to the user's `main_goal`,
`audience`, and `linkedin_level`. Present times and tactics as **general recommendations**,
not guarantees.

## Best Posting Times (general guidance)

- Weekday mornings and around lunch tend to perform well for professional audiences;
  Tue–Thu are commonly strong.
- These are starting points — tell the user to confirm against their own analytics after
  2–3 weeks, since their specific audience's active hours win over any general rule.

## Commenting Strategy

- Spend 10–15 min before and after posting commenting on others' posts in the niche.
- Leave substantive comments (add an idea, not "Great post!"); this drives profile visits.
- Reply to every comment on your own posts within the first 1–2 hours to boost reach.

## Networking Strategy

- Connect intentionally with people who match the target audience; add a short, specific note.
- Engage with a target account's content before sending a connection request.
- Build relationships with a few peers/creators for mutual engagement, not mass adds.

## Repurposing Strategy

- Turn a strong carousel into a text post and vice versa.
- Split a long article into 3–4 standalone posts.
- Refresh top performers every ~60–90 days with new framing.
- Pull comment discussions into the next post's topic.

## Engagement Loops

- End posts with one genuine question to seed comments.
- Ask a question in the post, then answer follow-ups in the comments to extend the thread.
- Use polls to harvest opinions, then publish a post analyzing the results.

## Weekly Review Metrics

Track weekly and review against the goal:

| Metric | Signals |
|--------|---------|
| Impressions / reach | Distribution health |
| Engagement rate | Resonance (comments > likes for reach) |
| Comments & saves | Depth of value (saves matter for authority/education) |
| Profile views | Curiosity → top-of-funnel interest |
| Followers gained | Audience growth |
| DMs / link clicks / leads | Conversion toward `offer` |

## Improving Future Posts From Performance

- Double down on the pillar/format/hook style of top performers.
- Diagnose weak posts: was it the hook, the topic, the timing, or the CTA?
- Keep a running swipe file of your best hooks and reuse their structure.
- Run small experiments (one variable at a time) and keep what works.
